How each part works

Fundamental score

Each of the 33 criteria from your Financial Ratio Summary scores Good = 2, Average = 1, Bad = 0, over the latest five fiscal years plus trailing twelve months. The score is points earned out of points possible, pulled a little toward 50% when few criteria can be measured, so banks and insurers (where debt, inventory and cash-flow ratios do not apply) are not over-rewarded.

Intrinsic value

DCF on EPS and on free cash flow per share: five years at the company's EPS growth (capped at 15%), then —% forever, discounted at —% (T-bill plus 5% equity premium). Peter Lynch value = EPS × (growth + dividend yield), capped at a P/E of 25. IV (FCF) is the median of DCF-FCF, DCF-EPS and Lynch; IV (cash) uses DCF-EPS plus net cash per share. Treat these as a ranking tool, not a price target.

Technical score and setups

  • Trend: swing highs and lows over a year (higher highs and lows = Up); a * means the trend is read from the 50/200-day averages instead.
  • Chart score: price above 20/50/200-day averages, 50 above 200, uptrend, MACD above signal, RSI 45–70, a bullish candle and a positive 3-month return add points; downtrend, RSI over 75, a bearish candle and a fresh bearish MACD cross take points away.
  • Alligator (Week 4): Jaw = 13-day smoothed average shifted 8 days, Teeth = 8 shifted 5, Lips = 5 shifted 3. The mouth is open when Lips, Teeth and Jaw stay in order and apart for the last 3 days; otherwise it is sleeping (no trade).
  • CWT Scenario 1: open, parallel Alligator and Heikin-Ashi candles above the Lips – buy-stop above the last swing high, stop below the Jaw. Scenario 2: mouth pointing down but price closes above the Jaw – buy-stop above the last lower high, stop below the fractal low.
  • Timeframes (Week 6, stocks): the Daily chart is the analysis timeframe and the 4-hour chart is for execution. This screener reads the Daily chart (free sources give no 4-hour PSX data), so confirm the entry on your 4-hour chart. The weekly Alligator is shown for information. A pullback into a bullish fair value gap above the Jaw gives a buy-limit instead.
  • Reversals (Week 7): double bottom/top, head & shoulders and inverse, rising/falling wedge, and RSI divergence (first swing below 30 or above 70). Entry on the break of the neckline or last lower high.
  • Continuations (Week 8): bull/bear flag, cup & handle and inverted, ascending/descending/symmetrical triangle, ascending/descending channel, and support↔resistance flips. Pattern projection is shown separately; the target rides to 1:3 reward:risk. A plan whose buy-stop is more than 10% above the close, or whose risk is over 15%, is marked “Extended – wait for pullback”.
  • Market filter: Alligator on an equal-weight KSE-100 proxy; the course takes buy setups only when the index is bullish on the daily chart.
  • Risk (RRMS): shares sized so a stop-out costs 1% of the capital set for you; the course's recovery ladder (0.15% → 0.35% → 0.70% → 1.5%) applies to intraday forex/crypto rather than this daily stock screen.
  • Candles checked on the last three sessions: doji, hammer, hanging man, shooting star, inverted hammer, engulfing, harami, morning/evening star, marubozu.
